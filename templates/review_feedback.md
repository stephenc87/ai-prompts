# Create Review Feedback

Turn notes about a document, design, project artefact, or piece of work into clear, constructive review feedback.

## Prompt

Using the review information I provide, write concise and professional feedback.

Structure the response like this:

## Review feedback

Start with a short positive summary of the work and its overall value.

Then provide the main observations as clear bullet points.

Each point should, where relevant:

* identify something that works well
* suggest a practical improvement
* connect the work to related project ideas or existing work
* explain why the observation matters
* distinguish current findings from future ideas or suggestions

Finish with a brief overall assessment that explains how the work can contribute to the project or be built on further.

Keep the tone constructive, specific and collaborative.

Do not invent issues, requirements, decisions, or project context that were not provided.

Review information:

Provide any relevant strengths, issues, suggested improvements, related work, technical observations, future ideas, or overall assessment. This may be structured or informal.
