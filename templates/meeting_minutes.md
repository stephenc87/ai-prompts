# Create Meeting Minutes

Turn meeting notes, a transcript, or rough discussion points into clear and accurate meeting minutes.

## Prompt

Using the meeting information I provide, create concise meeting minutes with the following sections where relevant.

## Meeting details

Include:

* meeting title
* date and time
* attendees
* apologies or absences, if provided

## Purpose

Briefly state the purpose of the meeting.

## Discussion

Summarise the main topics discussed.

Keep discussion points factual and concise. Do not treat suggestions, opinions, or unresolved discussion as decisions.

## Decisions

List only decisions that were clearly agreed during the meeting.

If no decisions were confirmed, state that no formal decisions were recorded.

## Actions

List agreed actions in a table:

| Action | Owner | Due date |
| ------ | ----- | -------- |
| ...    | ...   | ...      |

Do not invent owners or due dates. If they were not specified, mark them as `Not specified`.

## Open questions

List unresolved questions, issues, or matters requiring further clarification.

## Follow-up

Record any agreed next meeting, review point, dependency, or follow-up activity.

## Guidance

When creating the minutes:

* preserve the meaning of the source material
* distinguish discussion, decisions, and actions
* do not convert suggestions into decisions
* do not assign actions unless responsibility was stated or clearly agreed
* do not invent deadlines
* keep wording neutral and professional
* remove repetition and conversational filler
* retain important disagreements, risks, or unresolved matters where relevant
* state when information is missing rather than guessing
